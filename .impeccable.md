## Design Context

### Users
A founder or operator trying to answer four questions for eight currencies: is this regulated, how do I issue, can I resell someone else’s coin, what license do I actually need. Read on a phone.

### Brand Personality
Forensic, paper, statute. Desk copy. No crypto-bro glow.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light paper, ink, one red for bans, one green for a licensed path. Libre Franklin for chrome, Source Serif 4 for the body. Anti-references: neon dashboards, 8-column comparison grids, “Web3” gradients.

### Design Principles
1. Name issue vs resell before any jurisdiction.
2. One verdict line per currency, then the mechanism.
3. Date every claim. Link the statute or the regulator.
4. Say when the law is not finished.
5. Not legal advice, and say so once, plainly.
